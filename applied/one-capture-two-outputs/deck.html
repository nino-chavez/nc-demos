Ways of Working · Applied Rally HQ

One capture.
Two ways to teach.

A staged Rally HQ tournament journey can become a click-through guide and a playable video without inventing a second story for the product. The useful part is the attachment to real interface state: the teaching layer starts with the product, then adds an explanation a reader can follow.

02 The playable version

The video keeps the path whole

Some readers want to watch the setup unfold before they decide where to look more closely. The same staged create-tournament journey is available here as a video with its own controls. It has no spoken narration, so the surrounding copy carries the explanation instead of treating the clip as self-explanatory.

03 The source shape

The guide keeps each teaching step addressable

The interactive version needs more than a screen recording. A capture stops at each product step, saves a clean frame, and records the explanation and target area for that step. That makes a reader able to move through the same journey at their own pace, rather than scrubbing a video to find one choice.

05 The shared catalogue

Labels have one place to come from

Real state first

The product supplies the scene

Frames come from the demonstrated interface, not from a title card or a separately drawn mockup. The guide can point at a choice because the choice existed in the captured journey.

One catalogue

The catalog supplies the reader label

The homepage and static demo gallery both read the same four walkthrough definitions. The catalog holds the role, title, summary, step count, runtime label, and representative frame, so those two surfaces do not need separately authored labels.

This reduces one kind of drift: separate published cards describing the same journey differently. It does not make the capture immutable, prove an identical rebuild, or replace product testing.

06 The evidence boundary

The output teaches one staged path

This establishes a concrete teaching pattern: a product journey can support both a paced, interactive guide and a continuous video while staying attached to the same visible product state and reader-role catalogue.

It leaves important things unmeasured. These assets do not establish adoption, customer outcomes, speed or cost savings, current hosted status, or complete behavior across Rally HQ. They are produced examples of a staged journey. That is enough to inspect the teaching approach; it is not a substitute for evidence about the product in use.
